# Product Vision

## Purpose

Help everyday homeowners quickly create a simple digital version of their home so they can plan furniture placement, shopping decisions, and space utilization — without prior design experience.

## The problem

When people move into a new home, they need to buy sofas, beds, cabinets, TVs, dining tables, appliances, and storage. They struggle to answer simple questions:

- Will this sofa fit?
- Is this TV too large?
- Can I place both a dining table and an island?
- Will there still be enough walking space?
- How much storage can I add before the room feels crowded?

Today people estimate with floor plans, tape measures, or imagination. Existing interior design software is built for architects and professional designers — too complicated for everyday users.

## The solution

A platform where anyone can recreate their home layout in minutes and immediately visualize how furniture and appliances fit before purchasing.

**Goal:** smarter purchasing decisions using accurate dimensions and AI-powered recommendations — not beautiful interior design.

## Core philosophy

### Simple over professional

Designed for homeowners, renters, and everyday users — not architects or interior designers. Every feature should **reduce** complexity instead of adding it.

### Dimension-first design

Every object is a real physical object with actual measurements. Functionality over visual realism. The platform answers practical questions, not aesthetic ones.

### AI as practical assistant

AI explains recommendations and checks fit — it does not act as an interior stylist. Examples:

- "Only 5 cm of clearance remains"
- "The cabinet door cannot fully open"
- "The walkway is narrower than the recommended width"
- "Try moving the sofa 30 cm left to improve walking space"

Always explain **why**, not just **what**.

## Two modes of use

### 1. Empty house (bare shell)

User enters:

- Room dimensions (width, length, height)
- Doors
- Windows

Platform generates a simple digital layout.

AI can suggest circulation paths, standard clearances, and explain regional design standards (hallway width, kitchen aisle, shower door width, etc.).

### 2. Basic interior (finished home without furniture)

Furniture is simple geometric shapes — only dimensions matter:

| Item | Shape |
|------|-------|
| Sofa | Rectangle |
| Bed | Rectangle |
| Dining table | Rectangle |
| Cabinet | Rectangular block |
| TV | Thin rectangle |

L-shaped sofa = two rectangles combined. No textures, colors, or detailed models.

Platform suggests common sizes first (e.g. three-seat sofa: 180 / 210 / 240 cm), then user fine-tunes.

## Target users

- Homeowners
- First-time home buyers
- Renters
- People moving into a new home
- Families planning renovations
- Anyone purchasing furniture or appliances

**Not for:** architects, engineers, or professional interior designers.

## Long-term vision

Become the simplest home planning platform for better purchasing and layout decisions — complementing, not replacing, professional design software.

Users quickly recreate their homes, test furniture arrangements, optimize space, and shop with confidence.
